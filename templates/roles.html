Trees in Space Roles

Each Tree can wear several role tags. Every tab shows the Halo Infinite stats that matter for that role. Click a column to sort.

Lifetime matchmaking stats, per game. Flag, zone, oddball and stockpile columns are per game of that mode; “—” means no games of that mode. Gold marks the best in the tab. Tags are set with the checkboxes in the “Roles” sheet.
